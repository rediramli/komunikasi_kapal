Flash Report · D10 Real-Trace Replay

The Ultimate Dynamic Test — Replaying the Real PSA Agility Link Trace

29 October 2026 · Live testbed · Jetson LinUCB · tc netem replay of real vessel data

The static (S4) and synthetic-voyage tests showed the AI adapts. D10 goes further: we take an actual recorded trace of the Simba link from the PSA Agility vessel (RUT241, from the onboard router database) and replay it, second by second, onto our testbed SIM. No hand-picked numbers — this is what the real link did at sea.

The real segment

From the PSA link_stats_RUT241 table we took a contiguous 48-sample segment (~28 min of real sailing at the router's 36 s cadence) and time-compressed it to 3 s per sample (144 s replay). It is a genuine "flickering dead zone": a healthy ~30 ms baseline punctuated by RTT spikes to 1000–1864 ms and repeated demotions (100% loss), then recovery. Each sample is mapped to netem (delay = rtt − 15 ms, loss = loss_pct; a demoted sample → total drop) and held for its slot.

What the AI did — faithful to the real link

The AI shifted the camera load entirely to Singtel for the whole bad stretch (w=0 in 52 of 76 decisions), and returned to the normal split at the start and end when Simba was genuinely healthy. It tracked the real link, not a script — correctly abandoning a SIM that was spiking to ~2 s and dropping out, and reclaiming it the moment coverage returned.

Method

A reusable replayer, tasks/netem-replay.sh, reads the real trace (pushed to the RPi) and steps netem through it, holding each sample by re-asserting every 1 s (so OMR's tracker can't wipe it), with auto-restore on exit. The same 1 Hz telemetry and the live LinUCB controller run unchanged. Invoked:

ssh … 'cat > /tmp/netem_trace.csv' < trace.csv  then  ssh … 'sh -s wan1 3' < tasks/netem-replay.sh

Takeaway for the paper

  • D10 is the strongest validity argument: the controller is exercised by real at-sea link behaviour, not a model.
  • It behaved correctly under conditions harsher than our synthetic S4 — confirming the policy generalises.
  • Honest caveat for the write-up: this segment was so degraded that the "right" action was almost always "use Singtel"; a quantitative win vs baselines (goodput / video stall) on a mix of segments is the next step to show the AI's value, not just its correctness.
