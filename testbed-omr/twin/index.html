Digital Twin MPTCP

Camera stream from the RPi OMR gateway to the DigitalOcean VPS over two cellular links. When a link drops you can see where the traffic moves and how long the stream stalls. In LIVE mode the MPTCP scheduler can be switched one at a time and compared.

Simba Singtel

Cut drops all traffic on that link except to its own router (like losing the cellular signal); it restores itself after the chosen time, and only one link can be cut at a time so REDLAB keeps its SSH access. Primary path sets the MPTCP backup flag: “Singtel first” marks Simba as backup, so traffic moves to Singtel and Simba is only used if Singtel fails. Takes effect without restarting the tunnel.
